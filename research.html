Agriculture

farmdoc daily (weekly digest)

6 days ago · synthesized 2026-11-02

Nov 2: Cortarelli, Gardner and Colussi compare nine typical soybean farms (five US, four Brazil; agri benchmark data, 2020-2024). Total cost averaged $616 an acre in the US against $416 in Brazil, and the gap is overhead, mostly land ($251 against $97). Direct costs were close ($200 US, $218 Brazil): Brazil paid more for fertilizer ($85 against $57) and pesticides ($81 against $56), the US more for seed ($66 against $43). They conclude cutting inputs will not close the gap, and that Brazil's advantage narrows when imported-input prices rise (2023: $71). Five-farm and four-farm samples, so typical-farm illustrations, not national averages. Oct 29 (summary only): non-real-estate farm loan volume at commercial banks fell 25.0% year over year in Q3 2026 on smaller loans, while the number of originations rose.
