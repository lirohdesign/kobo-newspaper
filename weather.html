.DISCUSSION... Issued at 105 PM EST Mon Nov 16 2026

High pressure will edge east over the next several days. With the slow transition, this will lead to modest temperatures increases each day and possibly some lingering overnight/early morning fog/stratus issues. Xover temps will be met tonight as we head towards freezing once again and stagnant flow remains. Considered adding patchy fog, but soundings suggest it may be much more shallow and isolated in nature for many areas. As a result, left it out and deferred to evening shift to monitor trends.

A long wave trough, preparing to approach southern CA, will come onshore and eventually move NE into the Dakotas by 00Z Thu and the U.P of MI by 00Z Fri. The overall upper flow will remain southwesterly across the region, with a push of warmer air into the area. Highs will climb into the 60s for Thu-Sat. The area will remain dry being removed from the best moisture and lift well to the NW.

While the aformentioned trough heads NE, another upper low settles in further north, centering on Oregon by 6Z Fri. This feature will eventually eject towards the region to bring what may be the only chance for precip over the next 7 days. Timing looks to be Saturday evening into Sunday morning, but overall rainfall amounts likely will be on the lower side. In the wake of this front, a return to more seasonable temps occurs Sunday and then well below normal (low to mid 40s) by Monday.
