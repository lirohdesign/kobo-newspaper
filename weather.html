.DISCUSSION... Issued at 112 PM EST Sun Nov 22 2026

Near term forecast generally on track as a cold front continues to sweep across the area for the remainder of the afternoon into the early evening hours. A band of showers will persist (albeit in a weak state) over the next several hours, lasting for maybe 1 to 2 hours at any given location. Upstream rainfall amounts through 18Z have been even lower than forecasted, with most locations remaining AOB 0.05".

The first shot of cold air arrives in the wake of the front with 850 mb temps ranging from 0 to -4 C by 12Z Mon. Although a favorable NW flow will be in place and Delta Ts should be in the mid teens, low level moisture will be on the wane (925 mb RH at or below 70%), resulting in mainly some lake effect clouds, but no precip through Monday. A secondary trough arrives Monday night with additional cold air (-5 to -8 C by 12 Tue) sweeping in. Moisture remains very limited, but the combination of the strong trough and increasing omega may squeeze out a very narrow window of snow showers near the Lake MI shoreline Monday evening before moving into NE IL. High temperatures in the 40s and lows at or below freezing will remain entrenched in the area into next weekend with the coldest temps Tuesday and Tuesday night.

Models remain fairly consistent on a stronger northern stream low evolving in a larger longwave trough as it enters the Dakotas and dives SE to Iowa by 00Z Thu. A stronger vort max will translate in the southern stream with models varying on timing of phasing of this energy with the northern low. By 6Z Fri, EC/GFS agree on a 540dm-ish upper low located somewhere between western OH (EC) to SW Pennsylvania (GFS). Strong lift and moisture advection will take place as all of this is coming together with fairly high confidence on a band of rain moving towards the general area sometime in the Wednesday night to Thanksgiving day period with an extended period of wrap around precip possible into Friday before the upper low. Diurnally driven ptype issues will likely exist (rain/snow or all snow at night and just rain during the day), but plenty of time to sort out finer details in the coming days.
