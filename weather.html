.DISCUSSION... Issued at 120 PM EDT Fri Aug 28 2026

Quiet, pleasant weather will persist into this weekend as high pressure shifts off to the east and the mid level flow becomes increasingly zonal with little in the way of low level moisture advection or forcing. Low level flow will turn more to the south- southwest in this regime over the weekend allowing for a gradual warming trend. Several weak perturbations in the height field likely track east into the Great Lakes, one late tonight, and then another Saturday afternoon into Saturday night. Convection on the nose of a LLJ and developing MUCAPE axis likely remains to the northwest of the area with the main impact locally being an increase in mid-high level clouds with each passing wave. With that said, cannot rule out decaying elevated showers surviving into northern zones at some point Saturday night or Sunday morning.

Building heat and humidity then becomes the story next week, at least Monday through Wednesday, as a ~595 dm upper ridge at 500 mb flexes north into the Ohio Valley and southern Great Lakes. Current projections are for afternoon heat indices to reach well into the 90s each day, especially across the southern half of the forecast area where ridge topping convective cloud debris is less likely to spill over. Warm mid level profiles should put a lid on any surface based convection for the bulk of these periods, though cannot rule out convective outflow or elevated convection north of the US 30 corridor, especially toward the middle of the week. Models then diverge for the second half of the week on whether the ridging breaks down enough to send a frontal boundary either into or through the area with low chances (20-30%) for showers/storms and slightly cooler temps.
