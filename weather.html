.DISCUSSION... Issued at 1246 PM EST Sun Nov 8 2026

The pattern across the CONUS starts with a trough across the east and ridge in the southwest that waves are moving eastward overtop of. One such trough vacated the area this morning bringing subsidence into Monday. A brief period of warm advection later Monday into Monday night is overtaken by a brief period cold advection as another wave passes by to our north in the northwest flow. Not much surface cooling occurs as the cold front washes out across the area and it`s a dry frontal passage.

This series of waves has been able to affect the ridge in the west so that by the time the next wave overtops it, it`s able to dive farther southward. At the same time, an upper low in eastern Canada will help to push some cold air in behind this wave so that it`ll likely be rain at the start here later Wednesday. Then, we`ll have to watch for the timing of the cold air arrival and if there`s going to be some low level moisture to tap into. Lake effect precipitation could change to snow Thursday night into Friday as the cold air makes it to the surface. 850 mb temperatures get to -4 to -8C Thursday night, which is right around what`s needed to produce lake effect snow. Trajectories will also matter for where the snow bands end up. That will be out of the area by Sunday night or Monday as surface high pressure noses in. Those 850 mb temps don`t rise above freezing until we get to next week. GFS soundings are normally dry that far out so it`ll be interesting to see how the short range guidance handles it and this will help to address whether snow is heavy enough to overcome antecedant warm air. The NBM has about a 20- 30 percent chance of measureable (>0.1") of snow late Thu night-Fri AM. The NBM has high temperatures rebounding back into the 40s at least by the weekend and potentially as early as Friday meaning the snow should disappear quickly especially if that dry air remains in atmospheric column. Wind gusts also increase to 25 to 40 mph on Thursday with 25 to 30 mph gusts still possible Friday and Saturday.

Dry air takes over into the first half of next week after the cool/wetter air moves eastward as mid level ridging/surface high pressure slide southeastward.
