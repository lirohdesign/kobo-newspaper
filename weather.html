.DISCUSSION... Issued at 607 AM EDT Tue Sep 15 2026

A more unsettled pattern begins today as a low pressure system (over Ontario) brings a warm front through our area this afternoon, and then a cold front this evening into the overnight hours. This cold front will eventually stall out as the surface low/upper forcing lift further into Quebec. As of this writing, better moisture transport is just taking aim into our area. The warm front is currently draped from WI (nearly occluded) south into central IL and into TN/KY. Expect this to lift northeast through the afternoon. I suspect with additional moisture moving in towards the 12-15z time frame that we`ll see a few showers and maybe an isolated t-storm focused up near Lake MI initially, spreading eastward with a focus near the toll road. Most of the high res suggest these will taper off with time or even stay limited to the Lake MI area and near Cass/Berrien/St. Joseph Co, MI. There is still ridging aloft and high pressure at the surface, so how much moisture gets into this area early will determine how the showers evolve. The NAM is the most aggressive with this line of showers/storms extending from northern IL/Southern WI into the thumb area of MI, having it continue southeastward through the morning and early afternoon, gradually shifting it southward as it runs into the ridge. Other models keep it more limited and focused further north. For now, have pops of 20-50% for areas along/north of US 24, increasing with northwestward extent. Have around 55-65% for Berrien County, MI. Don`t expect any severe weather with this earlier activity if it survives. Right now from radar it looks as if the outflow is moving southeastward ahead of the storms, so that will certainly keep things limited (in addition to unfavorable timing given no instability/capping). SPC has our CWA in a marginal risk for strong- severe storms for the today/tonight period. This is a highly conditional threat as it will depend on how the earlier part of the day evolves. More showers and cloud cover in the west/north could limit our instability. Furthermore, the timing of the main cold front is slower than previous forecasts (around 00z its just southeast of Lake MI). We could see some storms popping around 6-8 pm ET in the west (perhaps lingering boundaries as well as any lake breeze could trigger things), and if they are able to tap into decent instability we could see a severe threat. However, the better potential really focuses between 8 pm ET to Midnight as the cold front moves in while we have some instability and the best forcing. The main threat would be damaging winds (SPC has 5%), with heavy rain and frequent lightning. ML CAPE looks to be around 1000-1500 J/kg in the evening (best west) and around 25-40 knots of 0-6 km bulk shear. Confidence is low (especially east of I 69), as we have potential for earlier showers/storms/cloud debris, paltry mid level lapse rates (5-6C/km) and the front is diminishing with time as it runs into the ridge. Have chances for showers and storms tonight in the 50-80% range. High temperatures will largely be in the low-mid 80s, with potential for high 80s, lower 90s further south and west. Heat indices look to be 95-99 degrees for these areas as humidity increases this afternoon...but confidence for any kind of advisory was too low given limited duration and also because if the more aggressive NAM is correct, we could have some showers/storms/cloud cover that limits daytime heating.

Shower and storm chances will persist through the weekend, with our lingering stalled front becoming more E-W oriented and flow aloft zonal to slightly troughed. This lowers overall forecast confidence as we are susceptible to any shortwaves that ripple through the flow, so timing is difficult to pin down. However, for Wed-Thu, have 50-70% chances that diminish to 20-40% Friday-Fri night. Additional potential returns as another cold front drops into the area Sat/Sat PM into early Sun, so have pops of 30-60 percent for the most part. Highs will be in the 70s, low-mid 80s for the majority of the period, with a couple days seeing upper 80s further south. By Sunday, highs are in the 70s.

Moderate swim risk for Lake MI beaches in Berrien Co, MI today. Breaking waves and currents are expected. Waves of 2-3 ft will subside to 1-2 ft later this afternoon. There will likely be a moderate longshore current moving south to north along the shore (as winds shift southwest), with moderate-strong structural currents on the south sides of piers and breakwalls. Rip currents are possible, but less likely given fairly shore parallel flow.
