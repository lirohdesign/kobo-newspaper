.DISCUSSION... Issued at 428 AM EDT Wed Sep 9 2026

A pre-frontal trough will cross the area from west to east today, bringing chances for showers and a few thunderstorms. SPC has areas generally along and south of US 24 in a marginal risk for isolated strong-severe storms, but confidence in this occurring is low. If stronger storms do develop, they will not be widespread. The main threat would be damaging winds, frequent lightning, and heavy downpours. Area in the marginal risk should have better instability as the feature moves through towards the mid-late afternoon hours (1500 J/kg), with about 20-30 knots of 0-6 km AGL shear (better shear arrives in the late afternoon, but it`s disjointed from the better instability). Plenty of moisture around with dewpoints in the 60s, 70s. High temperatures will be in the low to mid 80s along/north of US 24, and the upper 80s further south. Heat indices in the southern areas could reach into the low-mid 90s during peak heating this afternoon.

Southerly winds will strengthen slightly out of the southwest through the day, with a period of westerly flow possible briefly behind the passing trough. Waves on Lake MI around St. Joseph, MI northward may climb to around 2-3 feet by mid afternoon, and around 1-2 feet further south. Have a moderate swim risk for Lake MI beaches along the Northern Berrien County, MI shoreline today (including Silver Beach County Park and Tiscornia Park). Waves reach around 2-3 feet for Southern Berrien County, MI and La Porte County, IN by Thursday as well with northerly flow picking up slightly tonight into Thu. Have a moderate swim risk for both counties accordingly. Today, a moderate to strong south to north flowing longshore current is expected, which will lead to strong structural currents on the south side of piers/breakwalls. Rip currents are possible within the sandbars. All current types are possible on Thursday.

Becoming cooler and less humid behind a passing cold front on Thursday. Chances (30-50%) for showers and thunderstorms remain mainly along/south of US 30 during this period. Severe weather is not expected. It`s possible that the front reaches further south at a quicker pace than currently forecast, which would reduce pops for Thursday all together. Highs will be around 80 degrees, with lows in the 50s, low 60s.

While we will still have weak upper trough overhead on Friday (some models suggest more riding), surface high pressure remains in control with easterly flow in the low levels. Most of the guidance remains dry in this period, with the exception being the far south where some models produce light precip. Left out for now given low confidence. Highs will be around 80 degrees, with lows in the 50s, low 60s.

Highs warm back into low-mid 80s for the weekend into Monday, with an upper level ridge Friday night becoming more zonal with time, leaving us susceptible to shortwaves kicking off showers and storms. Have low chances (20-30%) each day through the period. Better potential (30-50% chances) arrive mid-late week as an upper level low drops into the northern plains and a broad surface low developing near IA deepens and lifts into Ontario/Quebec. This will bring a cold front through sometime between Tue-Wed. Highs remain in the upper 70s, low-mid 80s, coolest Wednesday.
