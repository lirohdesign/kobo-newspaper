.DISCUSSION... Issued at 106 PM EST Tue Nov 17 2026

A large upper level ridge will amplify into the Midwest and Great Lakes regions throughout the coming week with temperatures remaining very mild by mid to late November standards. Surface high pressure will prevail with little movement from where it is currently centered over the Appalachian mountains. One more night with lows around freezing is expected tonight, with clear skies and light southerly winds promoting at least some localized patchy fog for Wednesday morning. Otherwise, temperatures will continue to climb each day this week. Southerly flow and increased WAA will funnel in much warmer air from the Gulf and southern US. High temperatures will soar into the mid to upper 60s by late in the week, which is 15 to 20 degrees above normal for this time of the year!

As the ridge breaks down by late in the week, rain chances return by Saturday night into Sunday with the passage of a cold front. Given that this is several days away, the overall pattern evolution may change. There are still uncertainties as to how much moisture this front will have to work with so low to medium PoPs persist with 20- 40% chances. Following the frontal passage, temperatures will moderate back towards seasonable levels with highs in the 40s. There are no real signs of winter yet as the mild weather pattern looks to hold at least through the end of meteorological fall.
