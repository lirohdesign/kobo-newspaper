.DISCUSSION... Issued at 304 AM EDT Mon Oct 19 2026

Dew points were in the 20s and 30s this past weekend, but will be rising through the 40s today through Wednesday. This is as southwesterly flow Sunday and today promote moistening. Veering winds to easterly occurs Tuesday as a backdoor cold front approaches and washes out. As long as east winds continue Tuesday night/Wed AM and remain light, fog could occur as marine air is advected westward. A cold front approaches from the northwest Wednesday and may disrupt fog formation if clouds get to those areas early enough. This study of moisture content also begs the question about fire weather potential. Well, winds are expected to remain light enough to remove fire weather concern and limit that to grass fires at best. Given this mention of dry weather, rain chances will continue to be limited to less than wetting through most of Sunday. A cold front approaching late next weekend to early the following week (7-8 days away) would be the next chance to see rain, but it`s a small chance.

After starting this morning with a chance for frost, attention turns to tonight to see if we have another chance for frost. This is as long as the growing season remains intact. Given that dew points are expected to warm, low temps will have more of a floor to prevent cooling. Additionally, a cold front brings high clouds to the area Monday night limiting radiational cooling. Given these factors, have passed on issuing anything for frost Monday night. Lows stay more in the 40s for Tuesday night and beyond, meaning our next frost chance won`t be for a while now.
