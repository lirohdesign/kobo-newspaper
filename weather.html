.DISCUSSION... Issued at 345 AM EDT Wed Sep 30 2026

Not a whole lot of changes to the going forecast. Rain showers continue over northern IL into central MI this morning with the band of showers on the northwest periphery of ridging further south and east which will mainly keep the introduction of rain showers for areas closer to Lake Michigan into this afternoon as the lower levels further moisten. WPC has a marginal risk for excessive rainfall for later today and as the ridging pushes further south and east rain showers will make their way further south and east through Friday morning with a slight risk for excessive rainfall for tomorrow. A few embedded thunderstorms will be possible with the southern and eastward push but at this time severe weather is not anticipated.

A more zonal aloft pattern emerges across the region this weekend and will bring a return to dry conditions and cooler temperatures especially into the beginning of next week when highs will only get to around 60 degrees both Monday and Tuesday.
