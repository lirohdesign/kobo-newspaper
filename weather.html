.DISCUSSION... Issued at 212 AM EST Wed Feb 11 2026

A departing cold front paired with incoming high pressure, currently centered over Nebraska, promote cold air advection this morning. Stratus is moving off to the southeast such that steady temperatures in the mid-30s so far will plummet into the 20s momentarily. Cloud cover and high temperatures are areas of uncertainty today. As stated above, cold air advection is strongest this morning before 850-mb ridging gradually builds in to present neutral advection. Pair this with mostly sunny skies, and high temperatures well into the 30s are a likely outcome. On the other hand, if stratus returns this afternoon from moisture flux off Lake Michigan or from status upstream across northern WI and central MI, high temperatures could be closer to freezing. Strong subsidence from incoming high pressure threatens to lock-in any stratus that develops. No clear concensus on this in the model guidance, so, have stayed somewhat close to the existing forecast but offer a period of mostly sunny skies this morning followed by increasing clouds.

No significant hazardous weather concerns in this forecast package. The main story will be above-normal temperatures. We continue to monitor a developing low pressure over the Southern Mississippi Valley for the weekend that looks to present a 20% chance of rain, primarily driven by ensemble guidance. The parent trough responsible for cyclogenesis arrives onshore of the Baja tomorrow, thus, forecast confidence will be on the rise in subsequent model runs. High pressure over the Canadian Prairie and a departing upper- level low over British Columbia will influence the local storm track of the MS river valley low. This low generally emerges amid a broad ridging pattern across the central US which will push high temperatures into the 40s late this week and into the weekend. Early next week, highs in the 50s and even 60 appeared suspicious. However, amid all available model guidance, our advertised highs are within the 50th-75th percentile (e.g., with the 75th percentile, there is only a 25% chance of a high temperature warmer than 53 degrees on Tuesday). This increases forecast confidence for the temperatures forecast headed into next week.

Don`t proclaim winter is over yet...this is northern Indiana after all.
