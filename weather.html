.DISCUSSION... Issued at 208 PM EDT Mon Aug 31 2026

Main changes from recent forecasts was somewhat higher confidence in main axis of storms and impacts of heat/humidity Tue-Wed.

Scattered to at times numerous showers have dotted the skies through the morning, with the main area slowly starting to shift east. Additional development appears to be trying to expand in portions of NW IL to west central Lower MI in association with a weak mid-level wave. Models doing poorly on handling this, so confidence regarding evolution on the lower side. Suspect at least some CIN should be in place with the morning showers, but a pocket of clearing is noted that may help overcome some of that. Most likely this will remain west and north of the area, but trends will need to be watched for possible inclusion of pops in NW areas.

Focus then shifts to concerns for heat and humidity as upper level ridge builds north and shifts the axis of showers/storms into WI/MI through mid week. Models seem to be leaning towards this trend, but concerns still linger some given low confidence in model handling of subsequent waves and pops. Also, SPC has maintained the Marginal Risk for severe storms across the northern half of the area (possibly overdone). After much collaboration, it was decided to hoist a Heat Advisory from 16Z Tue through 00Z Thu for all of northern Indiana and northwestern Ohio where the greatest confidence exists for heat indices of around 100 degrees to possibly as high as 105 degrees. Greatest confidence does exist on Wed for criteria being met, but close enough to just go with both days. Concerns could linger across southern areas Thursday, but was decided to defer to later forecasts.

Thereafter, additional chances for convection and/or heat concerns will hinge on the position of the upper level ridge resulting in sporadic pops into the weekend and somewhat cooler temperatures.
