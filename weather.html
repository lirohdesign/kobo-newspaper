.DISCUSSION... Issued at 143 PM EST Sun Nov 15 2026

With the high amplitude ridge sitting over the central CONUS with a blocky-ish Omega flow pattern and extending well northward into central Canada, the northerly flow remains over the area and has continued to bring lake effect clouds downstream of the lake. With the slow progression of the ridge this may take a bit longer to get rid of the lake effect cloud production than originally expected. Mostly to partly cloudy conditions especially downstream of the lake looks to hang on into Tuesday morning before clearing skies amid a push of drier air moves into the area on Tuesday.

Dry conditions continue and a slow warming trend will take place into this workweek as the ridging continues to push eastward into the region. Highs in the 50s for Monday through Wednesday with a few locations getting close to the 60 degree mark by Wednesday afternoon and then highs into the low to mid 60s across the area from Thursday into the weekend. Mornings over the next few days will continue to be crisp with lows right around the freezing mark, however any areas with the low to mid clouds may stay a couple degrees warmer, so the low forecast will be predicated by the amount of cloud cover that is present for a given location.

Still a quite a bit of uncertainty in the longer term as guidance has been quick to dampen the ridging and perhaps bring in a disturbance just to the north of the area through the Great Lakes but this may be too optimistic and progressive in terms to what the actual outcome is.
