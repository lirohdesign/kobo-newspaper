.DISCUSSION... Issued at 256 AM EDT Sun May 10 2026

A large upper low was over James Bay. The upper level flow was topping the upstream ridge and was helping to tap into very chilly air deep into the northern Canadian interior. This cold air will be over the region and bring much below normal temperatures today and Monday. There is a good chance for frost tonight and Monday night. Given the advancing stage of vegetation and sensitive plants, frost headlines are likely to be needed for tonight and Monday night. The upper low will lift out Monday as a strong shortwave tops the upstream Pacific ridge and races southeast. Showers and scattered thunderstorms are expected to develop and move into the area Tuesday afternoon.

A weakening subsidence inversion will be over the area early Tuesday. The GFS profiles includes moderate shear but relatively limited CAPE values of generally less then 500 J/Kg. All considered, thunderstorms should remain below severe levels; however, strong gusty winds to 50 mph and hail up to 3/4 inch in diameter is likely with the stronger storms.

Lingering showers should end early Wednesday with a brief lull before the next system arrives. An upper level ridge will build over the area as short wave trofs continue to bring chances for showers into the weekend. High temperatures should rise well above normal from around 80 to 85 this coming weekend.

The combination of a low level front sagging south and smaller scale waves ejecting east out of the Mid MS Valley have allowed for persistence of some light rain showers and sprinkles early this morning, primarily south of the US 24 corridor. This should continue to be the case through daybreak as low level dry air advection continues to push south across northern Indiana. Diurnal mixing should allow for northwest winds in the 10 to 15 knot range today with some just low coverage diurnal cu expected. Another vort lobe rotating along western periphery of larger scale upper trough is likely to bring some additional VFR low clouds into the region tonight. Winds will become light northerly or variable this evening with loss of diurnal mixing as low level ridge axis builds in from the west.
