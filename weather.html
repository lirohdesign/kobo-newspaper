.DISCUSSION... Issued at 159 PM EST Sun Feb 8 2026

One more push of cold air behind the disturbance that brought this mornings light snow. Monday morning lows will sink down into the single digits mainly east of I-69 and in the upper single digits to low teens west of there. Fairly light southeasterly winds will help to keep wind chills warmer and more manageable.

Quiet weather is expected over the next several days with ridging over the central CONUS that will push eastward into the region on Monday and will start a much warmer (near normal) week compared to the much below normal temperatures that we have endured over the last several weeks. Highs this week will primarily stay around the low to mid 30s with Tuesday the warmest day with highs near 40 degrees. Of course, as mentioned in previous discussions the snow pack will keep temperatures cooler than what would otherwise be realized if we were free of snow on the ground. A weak system will move eastward north of our area and will not impact our area with any precipitation well north of our CWA. We will remain precipitation free and at least through Thursday when there is a possibility of some light snow showers with a weak disturbance moving into the area.
