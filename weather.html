.DISCUSSION... Issued at 1253 PM EST Thu Nov 12 2026

Now, with the area under a broad trough, various shortwaves dive southward through the Great Lakes region continuing to keep the lake effect machine invigorated into Sunday. During this time, the cold air at 850 mb lags behind and slowly drops southward finally reaching the lower Great Lakes Thursday night or early Friday morning. As a shortwave traverses Lk MI midday today, this pushes the lake effect rain area westward, into LOTs area. Some wavering of trajectories wiggles the main band eastward for a short period of time early Friday morning and possibly again around 00z Friday evening providing additional uncertainty to the forecast. Periods of drier air under the convective moisture column as well as DGZ moistening issues at times during this period likely impinge on rates and ratios even with some negative theta-e lapse rates and lake-induced instability to work with. The saturation issues could cause some drizzle to occur at times. If the band sits in any one spot in our area, it could snow, but then the rates are in question given questions about column saturation issues. The final vigorous vort max pushes through the area Saturday morning and this is also the time that 850 mb temperatures warm a degree or two and 1000-700 mb shear looks to increase by the time we get to Saturday and so even though trajectories attempt to gain a more northwest direction, many factors contribute to weaker snow, especially as trajectories waver minimizing the band`s residence time in any one location. Initially, it was looking like Friday`s snow was going to be the more impactful dose. However, if there is saturation issues through the column, then it may be Saturday produces more accumulating snow, but Saturday`s snowfall total ceiling is fairly low given the above points. Right now, only have a couple tenths of snow for either time period. Additionally, MaxTs are expected to reach back into the 40s both Friday and Saturday and those should limit accumulations especially farther from the lake. Roadways are starting warm from our recent above normal temp spell and it would take heavy rates to get them to accreting temperature. Given the above points, if it snows, it would probably be to grassy areas only.

As surface high pressure and mid level continue to build into the area from the west, trajectories gain more of a southerly component shutting off the lake effect machine and drying out the area. Given the place of the surface high overtime, a transition to a warm advection pattern takes over and high temperatures are able to gradually warm back through the 40s to the 50s by Tuesday or Wednesday. However, low temperatures overnight will be at or below freezing starting even tonight and won`t rise above 32 again until late week next week.
