.DISCUSSION... Issued at 134 PM EST Tue Nov 10 2026

Two jet streams are across the United States to start this period, with a Pacific Jet across the northern tier of the CONUS and a subtropical jet sliding eastward through the Gulf. After a dry day today, another wave overtopping the ridging in the southwest dives southeastward into the Ohio River Valley early Wednesday morning. It will attempt to tap into some moisture located across the Central Plains, but it appears that the warm sector will stay south of us or potentially get into our southern tier of counties (white to Jay County) with a couple hundred Joules of instability. SPC has the area in a general thunder outlook for Wednesday for the bulk of the area, but just a small area of a marginal for wind. Its a small period Wednesday afternoon/evening when severe components (mid level lapse rates and low level instability) have their best presentation. Otherwise, this area of low pressure is expected to supply some rain to the area starting late morning or midday into the afternoon Wednesday.

Then, as the main area of low pressure leaves the area, cold air wraps around its northwestern side and penetrates Lk MI Thursday night into Friday. At MGC, bufkit RRFS soundings indicate saturation occurs around 0z with around 20 to 25C delta Ts. As the cold air continues to filter south and eastward, bufkit RRFS soundings have snow beginning to mix in after 6z with all snow not too long after 12z and there are still some model differences in their handling of ptype. All of this lake effect precip stays west of IN-15 and most of it stays west of US-31. There will be some meandering of the band to some extent with some of the precip passing east of US-31, but most of it stays west. Surface temperatures are still forecast to rise into the low 40s as soon as Friday, which would indicate a change back to rain during the day Friday. However, the lake effect instability is expected to continue through Saturday and it is very possible that at least lake clouds continue for Sunday downstream. Some 20 to 25 mph gusts will also be possible Thursday within the gradient.

Trajectories finally rotate around to southwesterly by Monday and temperatures trend back through the 40s this weekend to achieving 50s on Tuesday. So below normal temps final become around normal by then.
