.DISCUSSION... Issued at 441 AM EDT Fri Sep 11 2026

Dry today into this evening with high pressure at the surface and upper level ridging remain in place. Expect partly to mostly sunny skies and highs around 80 degrees. Patchy fog is possible this morning, with a few locations showing less than 1 mile visibility. Don`t expect any widespread dense fog, and suspect it`s fairly shallow. If lower visibilities are reported persistently at a greater number of stations, may issue an SPS to raise awareness for those on the morning commute.

A trough moves through later tonight into Saturday as the upper level ridge meanders to the NE CONUS. Warm air/moisture advection kicks in overnight on the LLJ, bringing additional moisture to the area, especially further east. We could see showers with the warm front tonight into early Sat, and then again during the afternoon hours as a cold front (associated with a low pressure system moving into Hudson Bay) moves into our western CWA. The front then crosses into the overnight hours, settling just south and east of our CWA by morning. Suspect the best chances will be along/south of US 24, especially east of I 69. For now have 20-40 percent chances in this area, with a few thunderstorms possible Sat afternoon. Kept Sat PM dry for now, but if front moves through slower we could see pops shift further north and west and extend into Sat evening. Highs Saturday will be in the low-mid 80s (a few upper 80s west of IN-15). Severe weather is not expected at this time.

Another shortwave could move through somewhat zonal/slightly troughed flow on Sunday, perhaps lighting up convection along the frontal zone to the south/east of our CWA. For now have our area dry, but if the front is slower to exit we could see chances for showers/isolated storms return for areas along/south of US 24. Otherwise, on Sunday we`ll have breezy northerly winds (especially near Lake MI), so expect breaking waves of 2-3 feet and potentially strong currents along SE Lake MI beaches. Accordingly, have a moderate swim risk forecasted for both La Porte Co, IN/Berrien Co, MI.

Monday into early Tuesday afternoon should be mostly dry as an upper level ridge/surface high pressure build in over the Great Lakes. Highs will be in the mid-upper 70s, low-mid 80s. The best chances for any precipitation will be later Tue afternoon into Thursday (30- 65 percent). An upper level trough (potentially a closed low) will dig into the western edge of the exiting ridge during this period, with a surface low drifting from the northern plains Monday afternoon into northern Ontario/Hudson Bay by Tuesday afternoon. The trailing cold front will attempt to push through sometime Tuesday afternoon/evening and then stalls somewhere over our CWA-becoming a focus for more persistent activity through Thursday. The models disagree on the strength/exact position of the upper level ridge and whether or not the upper low remains and deepens (resulting in a stronger surface low) or if it will weaken and fill, resulting in a weaker surface system. It`s possible we stay dry until late Tuesday if the weaker low materializes and/or the ridge is stronger than forecast. We could see chances as early as Tuesday morning/early afternoon if the stronger upper low/surface system develops and/or the ridge is weaker than forecast and moves further SE. For now, have 30-50 percent chances Tue, and 40-60 percent chances Tue night into Thursday as the cold front moves in and stalls over our area. Thunderstorms are possible Monday night then again Tuesday afternoon into Thursday, especially during the afternoon hours. If the front makes it in, we`ll see gusty southwest winds shift north through the afternoon/evening, with waves potentially up to 4 feet on Lake MI beaches in La Porte Co, IN/Berrien Co, MI. Kept a moderate swim risk for now given lower confidence (2-3 ft waves), but definitely worth keeping an eye on for a potential beach hazards statement/high swim risk.

Highs Tue will be in the mid-upper 80s (especially if drier model solutions are correct), and then remain in the upper 70s, low-mid 80s for Wed-Thu. Friday will be coolest, with highs in the 70s.
