.DISCUSSION... Issued at 1240 PM EST Sat Nov 14 2026

With the north to northwesterly flow in place the pesky lake effect rain showers and drizzle will continue into this evening and areas near Lake Michigan may still see a few light rain showers/sprinkles into late tonight as drier air is entrained on the eastern side of the high amplitude ridging that is slowly moving eastward into the region. However, lake effect clouds will stick around through most of the day Sunday. Temperatures on Sunday will remain cool with high temperatures remaining mostly in the mid to upper 40s with the clouds keeping solar insolation in check.

But as the ridging continues to push eastward into the area a gradual warming trend will commence Monday onward with slight warming each day along with decreasing cloud cover into the beginning of the work week. Highs Monday through Wednesday will warm into the 50s, and by Thursday high temperatures will get back into the low to mid 60s into the weekend. A bit of uncertainty in the longer term as guidance is rather quick to break down the high amplitude ridging over the central CONUS which may be too progressive with this type of pattern.
