.DISCUSSION... Issued at 237 AM EDT Mon Sep 21 2026

Overall trend through the week will be for below normal temperatures and primarily dry conditions within low level east-northeast flow on the southern fringes of an expansive northern Great Lakes high pressure system. Still can`t rule out sporadic pockets of light showers or sprinkles today given lingering column moisture and another weak mid level disturbance drifting east atop the primary frontal zone now well south of the area. Best chances, 20-40%, for measurable light rain will generally be south of the US 24 corridor today. A more pronounced shortwave just entering the northern Plains does shift east over the area Tuesday into Tuesday night. Continued low level drying will keep any shower/sprinkle activity light and isolated/scattered at best during this time, with the bulk of the period dry and cool.

A more notable injection of dry air occurs mid week through the weekend as an upper level blocking pattern settles in with the local area generally located in a subsident regime on the backside of a developing Mid Atlantic negative height anomaly. More in the way of sunshine and gradual air mass modification should push highs back into the low to mid 70s by this weekend.
