.DISCUSSION... Issued at 525 AM EDT Mon Aug 24 2026

Quiet weather-wise today into Tuesday night/Wed AM, with partly to mostly sunny skies, light winds, and highs in the 70s, low 80s. Overnight lows will be in the 50s.

Northwest flow will keep waves on Lake MI in the 2-3 ft range for beaches along Berrien County, MI shoreline today. The higher waves will be further north as winds strengthen slightly there this afternoon. Conditions are still moderate swim risk this morning, coming down from high risk last night. Waves are around 2 feet with 5-6 second periods.

The surface high pressure/upper level ridge in place today and Tue will slowly drift eastward and break down through Wednesday, with an upper level trough crossing the Great Lakes later in the day Wednesday into Thursday night. This will bring a surface cold front through our entire forecast area by Thursday morning. Have chances for showers/storms increasing Wed into Thu AM from west to east, peaking around 30-60 percent for now (best chances west late Wed Aft/Eve). SPC added about 2/3rds of our CWA into a marginal risk for strong to severe storms...generally northwest of a line from Marion, IN up into Defiance, OH and onto Wauseon, OH.

Confidence is low at this time, especially further east mainly due to later arrival time-approx. 5-8 pm EDT Wed in the far west, then moving eastward as we lose daytime heating/instability. Mid level lapse rates aren`t all that impressive either (bordering on 6 C/km). Still, some of the guidance has mixed layer cape in the 1500-2500 J/Kg range further west in that earlier period, with about 25-35kts of 0-6 km shear. Dewpoints also reach into the mid-upper 60s, low 70s. As they mentioned in their discussion, sufficient for multicells and even a few supercells. Main threats are isolated large hail and damaging wind gusts, but with low LCL`s and a bit of low level wind shear (depends on the model), we could see tornado potential. At any rate, a lot could change in the next couple of days so will continue to monitor and refine the area of greatest risk. high temperatures will be in the upper 70s and low to mid 80s.

Dry conditions return Friday and most of Saturday, then low end (20- 30 percent) rain and storm chances are possible Sat night into Monday. Highs will be in the low to mid 80s.
