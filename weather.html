.DISCUSSION... Issued at 131 PM EST Fri Nov 13 2026

With a northerly flow continuing on the eastern periphery of high amplitude ridging over the central CONUS and extending well northward into central Canada, a band of lake effect precipitation continues to stream southward into northwestern IN and northeastern IL. Currently surface temperatures have warmed enough to keep all precipitation in liquid form. Tonight, a slight shift in winds to more northwesterly around 4 to 5 AM EST will allow the band to make a more easterly shift further into northwestern IN (northwestern portions of the CWA) and along with the diurnal cooling will start to see snow showers mix in especially further from Lake Michigan. Minor accumulations generally around a tenth to a trace of snowfall will be expected on grassy areas. After 10 AM EST, diurnal warming will once again switch precipitation over to mostly rain showers through the remainder of Saturday. Some of the showers will make it further inland by Saturday afternoon but will see a gradual decrease in coverage as winds weaken with eastward progression of the ridging into the western portions of the area by Saturday night into Sunday morning.

The high amplitude ridging will continue to slowly push eastward into next week allowing for a return to dry weather along with a gradual warming trend. High temperatures will remain in the mid to upper 40s on Saturday and Sunday with slight warming each day when Monday will get back into the 50s through midweek. By Thursday and Friday, highs will again push back into the low to mid 60s.
